# Teen Vaping in the UAE: The Law Requires ID Checks, Enforcement Has to Reach Every Seller

An awareness case study built from public sources only. No individuals, shops or schools are named.

**Read the study:** [Teen-Vaping-in-the-UAE-Case-Study.pdf](Teen-Vaping-in-the-UAE-Case-Study.pdf)

## The argument

In the UAE, vapes are legal for adults aged 18 and over, and selling them to anyone younger is a crime. The law already puts the duty to check the buyer's age on the seller. The open question is whether that check happens at every sale: in shops, on delivery and online.

**Recommendation:** check the Emirates ID at every sale, and bring the same age verification to delivery and online orders.
